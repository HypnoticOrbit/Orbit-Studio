About

Orbit Studio is a small desktop application for designing short vertical music animation videos, where glowing notes orbit around rings and build a melody note by note.

Once a video is created, the user can choose to send it to their own TikTok account as a draft, using TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. The user then reviews, edits and publishes the video themselves in the TikTok app.

How it works

  • Pick an animation style and a piece of music.
  • The Studio renders the video locally, on the user's computer.
  • Optionally, the user connects their TikTok account and sends the video to their TikTok drafts.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

1. Summary

Orbit Studio runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, no analytics and no advertising. We do not collect, sell or share your personal data.

2. Data used

  • TikTok access token: when you connect your TikTok account, TikTok gives the Studio an access token. It is stored only on your computer and is used only to upload the videos you choose to your TikTok drafts.
  • Basic profile information (display name and avatar, via the user.info.basic scope): used only to show which account is connected. It is not stored elsewhere.
  • Your videos and music files: processed locally. A video leaves your computer only when you send it to TikTok.

3. Data shared with third parties

The only third party involved is TikTok, when you choose to upload a video. TikTok's own privacy policy applies to content uploaded to TikTok.

4. Retention and deletion

You can disconnect your account in the Studio at any time, which deletes the stored token from your computer. You can also revoke access from your TikTok settings. Uninstalling the Studio removes all of its local data.

5. Children

The Studio is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly process their data.
